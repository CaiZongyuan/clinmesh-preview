# Spec: ClinMesh Preview — Agentic Mini HIS (Phase 1 Outpatient Encounter MVP)

## Problem Statement

Clinicians in outpatient practice spend disproportionate attention clicking through electronic medical record forms—transcribing symptoms, copying histories, drafting lab orders, staging diagnoses, and assembling structured notes—instead of focusing on the patient in front of them. Conversely, naive AI chatbots bolted onto a medical record either force the doctor to manually copy-paste suggestions or dangerously blur the line between unverified AI text and legally accountable clinical records. Meanwhile, teams building and evaluating agentic clinical workflows lack a lightweight, reproducible outpatient HIS workbench that enforces real clinical invariants (such as truth isolation during patient interviews, allergy red-lines, report revision re-acknowledgement, and server-verified encounter completion gates) without dragging in heavy multi-department hospital infrastructure.

## Solution

Build **ClinMesh Preview**, a lightweight Agentic Mini HIS focused on a single-doctor outpatient **Encounter** loop with an ambient, hands-free **Clinical Copilot** and a realistic **Simulated Patient**.

The doctor enters the outpatient workbench directly without multi-role login, selects one of four pre-prepared **Golden Cases**, and conducts a real-time spoken or text **Consultation** with the **Simulated Patient**. As the doctor speaks, real-time Chinese speech-to-text transcribes the utterance and sends it to the **Simulated Patient** (which responds strictly from its immutable **Patient Persona** via streaming text and optional interruptible Chinese speech playback, never revealing hidden **Case Truth**). In the background, the **Clinical Copilot** continuously observes the patient's **Visible Source History**, live **Consultation** stream, current interface state, and formal clinical facts, proactively updating **Draft** fields across the structured **Clinical Note**, candidate **Investigation** requests, candidate **Diagnosis** sets, and **Prescription** drafts directly through the UI agent bridge. Whenever the **Clinical Copilot** works on a clinical panel, an animated luminous halo makes its activity unmistakable without stealing focus, doctor-edited fields are never silently overwritten, and the assistant's detailed reasoning/chat rail stays collapsed by default. Every formal clinical decision—confirming a **Diagnosis**, issuing an **Investigation**, confirming **Report Acknowledgement**, issuing a **Prescription** or recording **No Medication**, signing a **Clinical Note**, and completing an **Encounter**—remains strictly gated by explicit **Clinical Confirmation** from the doctor and atomic server-side invariant verification. Once all five **Completion Gates** pass, the **Encounter** closes and unlocks read-only **Case Truth** debriefing for review and replay.

## User Stories

1. As an outpatient doctor, I want to open the workbench directly without registration or multi-role login, so that I can start practicing or demonstrating outpatient encounters immediately.
2. As an outpatient doctor, I want to view a queue of four pre-prepared **Golden Cases** with clear clinical tags and patient baselines, so that I can choose a realistic outpatient scenario to work through.
3. As an outpatient doctor, I want to start an **Encounter** for a selected **Golden Case** and read only the patient's **Visible Source History** and vitals, so that my diagnostic reasoning mirrors real clinical practice without seeing hidden answers.
4. As an outpatient doctor, I want hidden **Case Truth** (true diagnosis, unrevealed lab/imaging/exam results, and debrief notes) to remain completely inaccessible across both UI and API while an **Encounter** is active, so that neither I nor the **Clinical Copilot** can shortcut the diagnostic process.
5. As an outpatient doctor, I want to converse with a **Simulated Patient** via real-time Chinese voice or text inside a sequential **Consultation** stream, so that I can elicit chief complaints, symptom evolution, history, and red flags naturally.
6. As an outpatient doctor, I want the system to prevent submitting a second question while my previous question to the **Simulated Patient** is still awaiting a reply, so that the **Consultation** preserves a clean, auditable turn order.
7. As an outpatient doctor, I want the **Simulated Patient** to answer strictly from its version-fixed **Patient Persona** in realistic lay language without naming undiagnosed conditions or leaking lab values, so that clinical discovery feels authentic.
8. As an outpatient doctor, I want the **Simulated Patient** to reply with streaming text and optional Chinese voice playback that stops immediately when I begin speaking, so that I can conduct a hands-free spoken interview or mute audio in quiet environments.
9. As an outpatient doctor, I want to pause or mute the live clinic microphone at any time with a clear visual indicator, so that private remarks are not transcribed into the **Consultation**.
10. As an outpatient doctor, I want my **Consultation** transcript and agent sessions to survive page refreshes and transient network drops, so that I never lose clinical progress mid-encounter.
11. As an outpatient doctor, I want the **Clinical Copilot** to automatically analyze finalized **Consultation** turns and formal clinical events (such as newly released **Reports**) after a brief debounce, so that clinical documentation stays continuously up to date without manual prompting.
12. As an outpatient doctor, I want the **Clinical Copilot** to populate and refine **Draft** content directly inside the HIS workspace—including the six-section **Clinical Note**, candidate **Investigations**, candidate **Diagnoses**, and **Prescription** drafts—so that I rarely have to type repetitive clinical entries myself.
13. As an outpatient doctor, I want an unmistakable animated luminous halo to illuminate the exact clinical card or section whenever the **Clinical Copilot** is actively updating or has recently updated a **Draft**, so that ambient automation always feels transparent and trustworthy.
14. As an outpatient doctor, I want any **Clinical Note** field that I am currently focusing or have manually edited to be protected from silent AI overwrites, surfacing a non-destructive suggestion badge with one-click accept or dismiss instead, so that I always keep final authorship over my own notes.
15. As an outpatient doctor, I want the **Clinical Copilot** activity and direct chat drawer to stay collapsed by default as a compact edge status capsule showing live status and unread draft count, so that the main clinical workbench remains calm and uncluttered.
16. As an outpatient doctor, I want to expand the **Clinical Copilot** drawer whenever needed to inspect its live reasoning and UI tool traces, ask follow-up clinical questions by text or dedicated voice command, and collapse it back out of the way, so that explicit assistant interaction never crowds the main consultation mic.
17. As an outpatient doctor, I want every formal clinical commit to be physically impossible for the **Clinical Copilot** to execute autonomously and always gated by explicit **Clinical Confirmation**, so that no diagnosis, order, prescription, or signed note enters the official record without my approval.
18. As an outpatient doctor, I want to draft and formally issue **Investigations** across laboratory, imaging (text report), and functional exam (ECG/ultrasound) categories from a curated clinic catalog, so that diagnostic workups follow real order-to-report separation.
19. As an outpatient doctor, I want issued **Investigations** to resolve deterministically from the **Golden Case**'s hidden **Case Truth** (or bounded normal baseline rules when no specific pathology is defined) after a brief simulated turnaround with an optional instant-release control for testing, so that results are reproducible and never hallucinated.
20. As an outpatient doctor, I want each **Report** to be versioned so that if a lab or imaging department issues an amended revision, the previous revision and its acknowledgement remain preserved while the new revision reopens as unacknowledged, so that critical result corrections are never missed.
21. As an outpatient doctor, I want to explicitly perform **Report Acknowledgement** on the latest **Report** version rather than having passive viewing count as acknowledged, so that clinical responsibility for reviewing results is auditable.
22. As an outpatient doctor, I want to review and formally confirm a **Diagnosis** group from a curated ICD-10 catalog with a server-enforced rule requiring exactly one primary diagnosis and immutable revision history, so that diagnostic accountability stays rigorous.
23. As an outpatient doctor, I want to review and formally issue **Prescriptions** from a curated medication formulary only after a **Diagnosis** is confirmed, with hard server-enforced allergy red-line checks (such as blocking penicillin-class prescriptions for a penicillin-allergic CAP patient) that cannot be silently overridden, so that patient safety invariants are upheld.
24. As an outpatient doctor, I want to record an explicit **No Medication** conclusion (mutually exclusive with active prescriptions) for self-limiting conditions such as viral upper respiratory infection, so that responsible non-prescribing satisfies the encounter completion requirement without creating an empty prescription.
25. As an outpatient doctor, I want to review and sign the six-section structured **Clinical Note** (chief complaint, history of present illness, past & allergy history, physical exam/objective findings, assessment & rationale, plan & follow-up) as an immutable revision where subsequent edits append new signed revisions, so that formal clinical documentation is tamper-evident.
26. As an outpatient doctor, I want to see live status for all five **Completion Gates** (exactly one confirmed primary diagnosis, all non-cancelled investigations' latest reports acknowledged, valid prescription or explicit no-medication conclusion, signed clinical note, and zero unresolved high-risk drafts/suggestions) and have the server re-verify all five atomically when I submit **Complete Encounter**, so that incomplete clinical work cannot accidentally close an encounter.
27. As an outpatient doctor, I want the completed **Encounter** to freeze against further writes and unlock a read-only **Case Truth** debrief panel comparing my clinical record against the golden reference findings, so that I can review clinical accuracy after finishing.
28. As an outpatient doctor, I want to start a fresh independent **Encounter** for the same **Golden Case** after completion without overwriting or erasing prior encounters, so that I can replay cases and compare runs cleanly.
29. As a clinician using assistive technology or an accessibility/agent evaluator, I want every interactive control in the workbench to have a clear unique accessible name, proper keyboard/focus behavior, reduced-motion respect, and light/dark theme support, so that both humans and the UI agent bridge can reliably operate the interface.

## Implementation Decisions

- **Unified Edge Runtime and Embedded SQLite Topology (ADR-0001)**: Run the entire backend—HTTP/WebSocket routing, HIS clinical persistence, durable agent loops, and voice sessions—inside a single Cloudflare Workers + Durable Objects runtime with Hono as the router. Clinical HIS records (patients, golden case instances, encounters, consultations, drafts, investigations, versioned reports, report acknowledgements, diagnosis revisions, prescriptions, no-medication conclusions, signed clinical note revisions, and audit logs) persist transactionally in a workspace-level Durable Object's embedded SQLite storage (`ClinicWorkspaceDO`), eliminating D1/split-backend friction while keeping atomic single-transaction clinical commands.
- **Dual Isolated `pi-durable` Agents and Strict Truth Isolation (ADR-0002)**: Run both **Simulated Patient** and **Clinical Copilot** on `@earendil-works/pi-durable` inside separate per-encounter Durable Objects (`SimulatedPatientDO` and `ClinicalCopilotDO`), each checkpointed to its own DO SQLite store. `SimulatedPatientDO` receives only the immutable **Patient Persona** and doctor utterances, never receiving **Clinical Copilot** thoughts or **Case Truth** answers. `ClinicalCopilotDO` receives read-only clinical query tools (patient **Visible Source History**, clinic catalogs, formal encounter state, acknowledged/released **Reports**) and zero tools capable of reading **Case Truth** or mutating formal clinical records.
- **Default LLM Configuration**: Configure the `pi-durable` model layer via environment variables supporting standard OpenAI-compatible, Anthropic, and Gemini providers with independent model overrides for **Clinical Copilot** and **Simulated Patient**. Default to the local `pi` agent model `glm-5.3-flash` at `low` reasoning effort.
- **WebSocket Bidirectional `ui-sdk` Bridge & Draft Persistence**: The browser maintains the `@ui-sdk/react` `createAgent()` bridge connected to `ClinicalCopilotDO` over WebSocket. On finalized consultation turns (debounced ~1–1.5s) or formal clinical events (such as a newly released **Report**), the browser sends the latest `ui-sdk` snapshot plus the set of currently focused or doctor-edited field identifiers. `ClinicalCopilotDO` calls bridged UI tools (`ui_snapshot`, `ui_query`, `ui_act`) which route over WebSocket to the browser: the browser illuminates the targeted panel with an `ai-motion` animated luminous halo, diverts updates targeting doctor-edited/focused fields into non-destructive pending suggestion badges, applies safe **Draft** updates via `createAgent().act()`, and automatically blocks any attempt to invoke controls marked with `data-agent-confirm`. All **Draft** mutations debounce-sync to `ClinicWorkspaceDO` SQLite so refreshes preserve drafts.
- **Alibaba Cloud Bailian Voice Pipeline behind Cloudflare `agents/voice` (ADR-0003)**: Use Cloudflare `agents/voice` for WebSocket audio streaming, turn lifecycle, and interruption orchestration, wired to two custom Bailian adapters sharing a single `BAILIAN_TOKEN` credential:
  - `ParaformerTranscriber`: duplex WebSocket client for `paraformer-realtime-v2` (16 kHz mono PCM streaming, waiting for `task-started` before sending frames, emitting interim captions and finalized sentences on `result-generated` `sentence_end: true`, closing via `finish-task`), with an explicit local offline development fallback to browser Web Speech API when no token is configured.
  - `SambertTTSProvider`: WebSocket client for `sambert` (one-shot `run-task` with `streaming: "out"`, never sending `finish-task`, repairing streamed WAV sentinel lengths `0x7fffffbf` / `0x7fffff9b` on `task-finished`, supporting `AbortSignal` cancellation for instant barge-in interruption).
  - Preserve a `speaker: "doctor" | "patient"` attribute on every **Consultation** turn and an extensible speaker-attribution seam so Phase 2 can plug in real-clinic dual-human ambient listening and doctor voiceprint verification without changing clinical contracts.
- **Clinical Domain Model & Invariants**:
  - Four pre-prepared Chinese **Golden Cases**: (1) Community-Acquired Pneumonia with severe penicillin allergy red-line, (2) Atypical Angina / ACS workup with ECG ST-T changes and troponin report amendment, (3) Newly diagnosed Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus with UTI requiring primary + secondary diagnoses and dual prescription, and (4) Viral Upper Respiratory Infection validating explicit **No Medication**.
  - Single-doctor demo identity (`practitionerId` recorded on every formal write and audit event, ready for Phase 2 multi-role auth).
  - Server-side commands enforce all invariants atomically: single pending doctor turn in **Consultation**; exactly one primary **Diagnosis** per confirmation; allergy red-line hard refusal on **Prescription**; mutual exclusion between active **Prescription** and **No Medication**; immutable signed **Clinical Note** revisions; latest-version-only **Report Acknowledgement**; atomic five-gate **Completion Gate** verification before unlocking **Case Truth**.
- **Frontend Architecture & Visual System**: Built with React 19, TanStack Router, `@ui-sdk/react`, Tailwind CSS v4 (declaring `@layer ui-sdk;` above Tailwind and importing `@ui-sdk/core/web.css` in `layer(components)`), and `ai-motion` for the luminous agent halo. Adopts ClinMesh's calm medical light/dark color tokens (`color-scheme: light dark` with `light-dark()` and fallback), reduced-motion opt-in rules, a three-column outpatient workbench (collapsible left golden-case queue & patient summary, center HIS workspace with top live voice bar, right **Clinical Copilot** activity/chat drawer collapsed by default).

## Testing Decisions

A good test verifies **external observable behavior and clinical invariants** through real boundaries rather than asserting internal helper calls, mocks, or private implementation details. Every vertical slice lands with tests at the **two highest system-boundary seams**:

1. **Worker Edge Seam (Backend Integration & Domain Tests)**: Exercise the real Hono HTTP/WebSocket entry running against real Durable Objects and real embedded SQLite (Cloudflare Workers Vitest pool / Miniflare), injecting test doubles **only** at the two external I/O boundaries:
   - Scripted **LLM Provider** seam (running the real `pi-durable` loop, SQLite checkpoint recovery, tool execution, and context isolation between `SimulatedPatientDO` and `ClinicalCopilotDO`).
   - Scripted **Bailian Voice Adapter** seam (`Transcriber` and `TTSProvider` driving the real `agents/voice` session lifecycle, sentence finalization, WAV sentinel header normalization, and interruption cancellation).
   - Verifies all clinical invariants: `Case Truth` non-leakage before completion, sequential consultation turns, allergy red-line hard blocks, report amendment invalidating prior acknowledgement, mutual exclusion of prescriptions and **No Medication**, immutable signed note revisions, atomic five-gate completion enforcement, and replay creating an independent new **Encounter**.
2. **Browser Workbench & `ui-sdk` Agent-Ready Seam (Frontend Tests)**: Render the real TanStack Router + `@ui-sdk/react` workbench in jsdom with Testing Library and a real `createAgent()` bridge, stubbing only the outermost HTTP/WebSocket transport and browser audio APIs.
   - Follows the `ui-sdk` agent-ready testing standard: asserts every visible control has a unique non-empty label, verifies `agent.act()` can populate **Draft** fields across clinical modules while respecting doctor-edited field conflict protection, verifies `ai-motion` glow state attributes activate/settle properly, and proves `data-agent-confirm` refuses every formal clinical commit when unapproved and succeeds once explicitly approved.

## Out of Scope

- Multi-role hospital staff workflows: Registration clerk, Triage nurse, Cashier/billing, onsite Pharmacy inventory/dispensing, Inpatient admissions/wards/beds, Surgery/materials, and Medical Record archiving/DRG payer reviews.
- Real-clinic dual-human ambient room listening and biometric doctor voiceprint enrollment/verification (explicitly deferred to Phase 2; only the `speaker` field and attribution seam are reserved in Phase 1).
- User registration, login flows, and production RBAC administration (Phase 1 uses a single pre-configured demo doctor while recording `practitionerId` on every audited write).
- Real external LIS/PACS/DICOM pixel viewers, pathology slide viewers, real FHIR server ingestion, or real insurance claims interfaces.
- Dynamic LLM generation of brand-new cases at runtime (Phase 1 ships four immutable curated **Golden Cases**).
